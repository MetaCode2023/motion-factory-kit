# Taste: what the owner wants in every video

Standing preferences learned from review notes. Read it before writing a brief; it beats the kit's defaults.
When a note generalises, add it here with the date and where it came from. When a rule changes, edit it in place
and say so in `docs/DECISIONS.md`. Newest first inside each section.

## How to edit (above all)

- **Reason, don't transcribe.** Raw footage and notes are raw material. Find the one idea, restructure, cut, add
  depth, explain your calls afterwards. Turning someone's notes straight into a video was the wrong call.
  (2026-11-19, owner's critique of the early builds; see docs/EDITORIAL.md)
- Decide by default; ask only before spending credits or posting. (2026-11-19)

## Content

- Deep and current, never generic: go to the profound version of the idea (e.g. quantum: superposition,
  measurement and collapse, Copenhagen vs Many Worlds, the observer), but earn every term so a first-time viewer
  follows. Think "polymath explaining it to a smart friend". (2026-11-19, quantum B-roll test; PLAYBOOK section 0)
- Cold-open hook, a promise in the first 15 s, a question for the viewer, an end card, and vertical Shorts cut from
  the long video. (2026-11-17, Physics of Poker v1 notes)

## Picture

- Never just a talking head: something to look at every second. Full-screen graphics put the speaker in a circle in
  the corner (`layout: pip`); otherwise side by side (`layout: split`). (2026-11-17)
- B-roll throughout, and **real video of the actual subject** over photos with a zoom. Check stills: titles lie.
  Credit every clip. (2026-11-19, quantum test; docs/LESSONS.md)
- News-style outside clips (TV frame, outlet lower third) are wanted, with the owner signing off per clip.
  (2026-11-18)

## Captions

- Burned in for the whole video, not just an .srt. (2026-11-17)
- Key words pop big in the accent colour; main lines get big quote cards. Liked. (2026-11-17, confirmed 2026-11-19)

## Sound

- Popular short-form effects (page turns, beeps, boops, pops, swipes), one per beat, plus music under everything.
  (2026-11-19)
- Narration throughout when there's no on-camera speaker. (2026-11-19)
