# Run report: "Order #42" (a bare creative prompt, no footage), 2026-11-24

**The prompt (Austin, as a benchmark):** "Make me a video about being a normie driving through Taco Bell and
suddenly getting hit with expansive thinking about my own mind, the universe, and then just snapping back to the
base reality I was in but now with an intriguing little glimmer in my eye."

**What came out:** a 73 s vertical Short, `scenes/mine/order-42/`. A guy at the drive-thru speaker is asked
"anything else?", falls through his own eye into his neurons, the stars and the start of time, is honked back, says
"No. That's everything." His receipt reads ORDER #42; the last frame is one gold glint in his eye. Plan, script and
design rules: `scenes/mine/order-42/PLAN.md`.

## How it was made (so the next one is faster)

1. Hook options + script + a shot-by-shot plan with a design system (the arrow-of-time format: a signature motif,
   type that behaves, a colour arc, sound design), posted before building.
2. Voice: Higgsfield `seed_audio`, **one take per character** (him: ~140 words, 4.5 credits; the drive-thru worker:
   5 short lines, ~1.5 credits). Asked first. One take each is cheaper than one per line and keeps the delivery even.
3. `prep.py` transcribes both takes (tools/transcribe.py), cuts them into lines at the middle of each pause, treats
   them (the worker gets an intercom band-pass + grit; "anything else?" gets an echo tail that never ends), lays them
   on a timeline and writes every story cue (`timeline.js`) from the real word times. The scene and the score are
   both cut to those cues.
4. `sound.py` synthesizes the score and effects with numpy (lo-fi car radio, the radio filtering away, a cosmic pad,
   dead stop on a horn, the radio resuming *where it left off*, receipt printer, sparkle). No samples, no credits.
5. Images: real NASA/ESA/Webb/Hubble and CC photos through Openverse (the only free-media source the cloud reaches).
6. Scene: everything else drawn in code (the drive-thru, the speaker, the car, the window, a 5x7 dot-matrix font).
7. Build, read the report and cuts sheet, fix, rebuild. Render: 2,204 frames in ~2 min on 3 cores.

## Self-grade

**Did well without help:** turned a one-sentence vibe into a structured joke with a real idea under it (the self as
86 billion neurons with nobody in charge; Sagan's "a way for the cosmos to know itself"), facts checked (the first
draft said "every atom" was made in stars: wrong, hydrogen is from the Big Bang; changed to iron and carbon). Made a
design system that carries meaning (dot-matrix = the mundane, drifting type = the trip, circles as every cut, the
clock reading 11:47 before and after). Kept every brand mark out. Timed everything to the real voice. Asked before
spending credits.

**Where it needed hand-holding or fell short:**
- **No real video B-roll.** The cloud can't reach Pexels, NASA video, Pixabay or archive.org; Wikimedia 429s; the
  laptop's file VM has no network. Only Openverse stills (≤1024 px) were reachable, so the cosmic shots are stills
  pushed 5-6x and look soft. The fix is to run pulls on the laptop (Remote Control), or to generate (credits).
- **The tools needed patching mid-run:** `tools/transcribe.py` crashed (faster-whisper vs PyAV version mismatch);
  `make.mjs` had no way to take real word timings for a voice *file*, so its overlap check counted silent tails and
  echo as speech. Both fixed in this PR.
- **The kit's starting point is stale:** LESSONS.md, STATUS.md, caption emphasis, the voice-line splitter and the
  film-look layer all live on unmerged branches (#5, #7, #2, #19). An agent starting from `main` doesn't see them and
  rebuilds them (this run rewrote a line splitter and its own caption emphasis). Merging those is the single biggest
  improvement to "how well does it do from a bare prompt".
- **The picture score's safe-zone check flags full-bleed imagery as text** ("unsafe ink" ~1.0 in every zone of a
  starfield). It should measure text-like strokes, not ink.
- The drawn world (car, speaker, window) reads as clean motion graphics, not photoreal. That fits the joke but
  isn't "cinema". A generated 5 s drive-thru plate would lift the first 18 s the most.
- Script-first: Austin's standing preference is to approve the script before a render. This run built in parallel
  (the build costs nothing and the brief was a benchmark of the whole system), and said so; the script was open for
  notes the whole time.
