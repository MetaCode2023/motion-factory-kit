# Brief: physics-of-poker

Worked example for the demo cut (`physics-of-poker.json`, two short podcast clips). Written before the plan;
the plan follows it. Guide: docs/EDITORIAL.md. Evidence: `node longform.mjs analyze podcast-ep1` and `podcast-ep2`.

## The one idea
A good poker player thinks like a physicist: an opponent's hand is a spread of possibilities until a bet "measures" it,
and bluffing works by making every measurement look the same.

## Who it's for and what they already know
Curious people who have played a home game and never heard the word superposition.

## Hook (first 3-10 s)
`podcast-ep1#1-#3` "Here's the thing about poker that nobody tells you... your opponent's hand is not one hand. It's
every hand at once." The map ranks `#17` ("every bet is a measurement") higher, but it's the payoff: opening on it
spends the turn before the viewer cares. `#1-#3` makes a promise (a secret) and a strange claim in 15 s.

## Structure (story order, not recording order)
1. Hook: ep1 #1-#3, the strange claim.
2. The term, earned: ep1 #4-#7 (superposition, said by the speaker, shown with the cloud of hands).
3. Back to the table: ep1 #8-#11, what a good player sees.
4. The turn: ep1 #12-#17, the river as a measurement (the collapse animation lands on "Boom").
5. Payoff: ep1 #18-#19, then ep2 #2-#9 on bluffing as making every observation look the same.

## Cut, and why
- ep2 #1 "Okay so let's talk about bluffing": table talk; the chapter card does its job.
- ep1 #19 false start "They're running um": the restart says it cleanly.
- Fillers and pauses over 0.45 s: ~20 s of 2:00 raw.

## Added depth (not in the footage)
- "Wave function" is said but never defined: the collapse animation shows it (spread -> one spike) instead of a definition card.
- Outside proof: a televised final-table moment and a famous bluff, as pulls for the creator to approve.

## Formats
Long-form 16:9 demo, ~1:34. Short candidate: ep2 #2-#9 (bluffing, ~35 s, opens on a self-contained line).
